This tool reads your official Instagram data export. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded, no login, no password, no risk to your account.

How to get your Instagram data (≈2 min)
  1. Open Instagram → Profile → ☰ Menu → Accounts Center.
  2. Go to Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  3. Choose Download or transfer information → your account → Some of your information.
  4. Under Connections, select Followers and following.
  5. Set Format: JSON and Date range: All time, then request the download.
  6. Instagram emails you a .zip (usually within minutes). Upload it below.

No ZIP yet? You can also drop the individual followers_1.json and following.json files (found under connections/followers_and_following/).
